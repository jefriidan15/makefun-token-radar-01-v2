How radar-2 works

Implied value = TVL × comparable multiple × growth × maturity × breadth. The multiple is the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of live-token protocols in the same category (3+ peers, else the global median), clamped to 0.05–0.60. Growth adds half the 7-day TVL change (clamped −60% to +120%). Maturity rises with age up to 3 years. Breadth adds up to 10% for multi-chain protocols. Only protocols that record no token rank; live-token protocols are comparables, never ranked.
